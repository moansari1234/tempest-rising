# ✝️ ARC 8: HOLY EMPIRE RUBERIOS & THE CLASH OF DRAGONS
### *(聖魔対立・神聖法皇国編 - Seima Tairitsu / Ruberios-hen)*

---

## ⛪ 1. The Seven Celestial Sages' Conspiracy
* **The Puppet Masters of the Church**: The **Seven Celestial Sages (七曜の老師)**, ancient human advisors within the Western Holy Church, fear losing their religious monopoly over the Western Nations to the booming multiracial metropolis of Tempest.
* **The Forged Message**:
  * The Sages tamper with the communication crystal between Rimuru and Holy Knight Captain **Hinata Sakaguchi**, replacing Rimuru's peaceful invitation with an aggressive declaration of war.
  * Hinata leads her elite Holy Knight Paladins toward the borders of Tempest to seek a decisive resolution.

---

## ⚔️ 2. The Second Duel: Rimuru vs. Hinata Sakaguchi
* **The Terms of Battle**: To prevent total war between their armies, Rimuru and Hinata agree to a 1-on-1 duel to settle the dispute.
* **Hinata’s Arsenal**:
  * Wields the Holy Sword *Moonlight* and the Dragon Buster armament.
  * Unleashes her ultimate strike: **Melt Slash (崩魔霊子斬)**, which condenses the ultimate holy light of *Disintegration* onto her sword blade to vaporize everything it touches.
* **The Devour of Melt Slash**:
  * Wisdom King Raphael calculates the trajectory; Rimuru sacrifices 70% of his magicule body mass to swallow the entire *Melt Slash* directly into **Beelzebuth**, analyzing the fundamental spiritron laws of Holy Magic in the process.
  * Acknowledging Rimuru's overwhelming superiority, Hinata sheathes her blade and yields.

---

## 🩸 3. The Seven Sages' Execution & Luminous Valentine’s Arrival
* **The Sages' Last Ambush**:
  * The Seven Sages launch an illegal nuclear-grade holy magic blast from behind to assassinate both Hinata and Rimuru simultaneously.
  * **Diablo** arrives on the battlefield, catching the blast with a single finger and terrifying the corrupt priests into madness.
* **The True God Revealed**:
  * The Vampire Queen and Demon Lord **Luminous Valentine** descends upon the field in full gothic splendor, exposing the Sages' betrayal of her doctrine.
  * Luminous uses *Sanctuary Disintegration* to permanently erase the traitorous Sages from existence.

---

## 🤝 4. The Tempest-Ruberios Peace & Economic Bridge
* **The Secret Truth**:
  * Luminous confesses to Rimuru that she created the Western Holy Church to cultivate humanity peacefully as blood livestock and worship energy under strict vampire protection.
  * Recognizing each other’s pragmatism, Rimuru and Luminous formalize an official non-aggression and mutual defense pact.
* **Cultural & Technological Exchange**:
  * Tempest engineers construct high-speed paved highways and the **Magical Train Transit Railway** linking the Jura Forest directly to Holy Empire Ruberios.
  * The Holy Church officially amends its holy scripture, recognizing Tempest as a blessed nation of peaceful demi-humans.
