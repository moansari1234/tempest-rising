# 🎪 ARC 9: THE TEMPEST FOUNDERS FESTIVAL & THE LABYRINTH
### *(開国祭・迷宮公開編 - Kaikokusai / Meikyū Kōkai-hen)*

---

## 🎉 1. The Grand Tempest Opening Festival (開国祭)
* **The Global Invitation**: Rimuru invites royalty, merchants, guild masters, and monarchs from across the Cardinal World to the grandest expo in history in **Tempest City**.
* **Distinguished Guests**:
  * King Gazel Dwargo (Dwargon), Empress Elmesia (Sarion), Demon Lord Luminous Valentine, Duke Myourmiles (Ingrassia Merchant Coalition), and the Free Guild representatives.
* **The Cultural Revelations**:
  * Classical Japanese orchestra concerts conducted by Shuna.
  * Multi-course gourmet banquets featuring sushi, tempura, ramen, and refined alcohol brewed in Tempest.
  * High-tech exhibits: glass lanterns, magi-steel metallurgy, and geothermal hot spring spas.

---

## 🗡️ 2. The Martial Arts Tournament of Champions
* **The Grand Arena**: A colossal coliseum hosting a tournament between world champions and Tempest officers.
* **Gobta & Ranga's Fusion**:
  * Gobta uses *Shadow Step* to summon Ranga and accidentally fuses into **Gobta (Star Wolf Armor Form)**, knocking out high-tier knights with ridiculous luck and speed.
* **Masayuki the "Lightspeed Hero"**:
  * An Otherworlder high schooler, **Masayuki Honjo**, arrives with the **Unique Skill: Chosen One (英雄覇道)**, which causes everyone to mistake him for an invincible legendary hero through reality-bending charisma.
  * Rimuru recruits Masayuki as Tempest’s chief public ambassador.

---

## 🏰 3. Construction of the 100-Floor Dungeon Labyrinth
* **The Ramiris Collaboration**:
  * Fairy Demon Lord **Ramiris** moves her entire domain to Tempest.
  * Together with Rimuru and Veldora, they construct a spatial multi-dimensional **100-Floor Dungeon Labyrinth (地下迷宮)**.
* **The Safety Bracelet (蘇生の腕輪 - Bracelet of Revival)**:
  * Adventurers exploring the Labyrinth wear magical resurrection bracelets. Upon taking lethal damage, they are instantly teleported to the surface with full HP, creating an infinite, death-free challenge loop!
* **The Labyrinth Hierarchy & Guardians**:
  1. **Floor 50**: Boss Chamber (Bovix & Equix).
  2. **Floor 70**: **Adalman (Gehenna Lord)** commanding skeletal legions and the Death Dragon Wenti.
  3. **Floor 79**: **Apito (Insect Queen)** guarding glowing amber pollen groves.
  4. **Floor 80**: **Zegion (Mist Lord - Emperor of the Labyrinth)**, an invincible bio-metallic beetle warrior infused with Rimuru’s flesh and magisteel armor.
  5. **Floor 90**: **Kumara (Chimera Lord)** commanding eight mythical beast avatars.
  6. **Floor 100**: The Throne Room of **Storm Dragon Veldora**, the ultimate final boss of the labyrinth!

---

## 💰 4. The Economic & Industrial Revolution
* **The Adventurer Boom**: Thousands of adventurers, guilds, and merchants flood into Tempest daily, paying entrance fees, buying recovery potions, renting hotels, and harvesting dungeon loot.
* **Tempest as the Center of the World**:
  * Tempest establishes the international reserve currency based on Dwarven gold bullion and Magisteel reserves, cementing Rimuru's nation as the supreme economic, cultural, and military capital of the Cardinal World.
