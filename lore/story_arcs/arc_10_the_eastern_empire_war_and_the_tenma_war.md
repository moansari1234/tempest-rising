# 🎖️ ARC 10: THE EASTERN EMPIRE WAR & THE GREAT TENMA WAR
### *(帝国侵略・天魔大戦編 - Teikoku Sensō / Tenma Taisen-hen)*

---

## 🚀 1. The 1,000,000-Man Eastern Empire Invasion
* **The Imperial Army**: Emperor **Rudra Nam Ul Nasca** launches a full-scale invasion of the Western Nations with a terrifying military machine:
  * 1,000,000 magically enhanced cyborg soldiers, magi-tech tank battalions, and a fleet of 300 flying dreadnought airships armed with magicule laser cannons.
* **The Labyrinth Meat Grinder**:
  * Instead of engaging on the surface, Rimuru relocates the entire city of Tempest inside the Labyrinth and lures 700,000 imperial troops into the dungeon.
  * **The Annihilation**: The Guardian Lords massacre the invasion force without a single Tempest casualty:
    * **Zegion** shreds entire divisions with *Dimension Ray*.
    * **Adalman** and **Kumara** obliterate armored brigades with gravitational pulses.
    * **Diablo** and the Three Demoness Primordials (Testarossa, Ultima, Carrera) dismantle the imperial elite with zero effort.

---

## 👑 2. Awakening the Twelve Guardian Lords (テンペスト十二守護王)
* **The Harvest of 700,000 Souls**:
  * Rimuru harvests the souls of the fallen imperial army and uses **Harvest King Shub-Niggurath** to distribute **100,000 souls** each to his 12 greatest subordinates.
* **The Mass True Demon Lord Awakening**:
  * **Benimaru** (Flare Lord), **Shion** (War Lord), **Diablo** (Demon Lord), **Ranga** (Star Lord), **Souei** (Darkness Lord), **Geld** (Barrier Lord), **Gabil** (Vortex Lord), **Zegion** (Mist Lord), **Apito** (Insect Queen), **Kumara** (Chimera Lord), **Adalman** (Gehenna Lord), and **Carrera/Testarossa/Ultima** all ascend to **True Demon Lord tier (Awakened Calamities)** simultaneously!

---

## 🐉 3. The True Dragon War: Rimuru vs. Veldora & Velgrynd
* **The Scorch Dragon's Ambush**: The Scorch Dragon **Velgrynd** and Emperor Rudra use the Ultimate Skill *Justice King Michael* to dominate Veldora’s mind, capturing him.
* **Rimuru's Divine Rage**:
  * Seeing his sworn brother enslaved, Rimuru undergoes his ultimate emotional evolution, breaking all cosmic limits.
  * Raphael evolves into **Manas: Ciel (神智核 - Ciel)**, achieving complete sentient consciousness and boundless computational divinity.
* **Devouring Two True Dragons**:
  * Ciel routes Rimuru's power into *Beelzebuth*; Rimuru consumes both **Veldora** and **Velgrynd** simultaneously into his *Imaginary Space*, freeing Veldora and liberating Velgrynd’s dragon factor.
* **Ascension to Ultimate Slime (Dragonoid Demon God)**:
  * Rimuru integrates the True Dragon factors, evolving into an **Ultimate Slime (Viscous Draconic Demon God - 竜魔粘性星神体)** and awakening **Ultimate Skill: Void God Azathoth (虚空之神)**.

---

## 🌌 4. The Great Tenma War (天魔大戦)
* **The Final Cosmic Confrontation**:
  * The Ancient Supreme Angel **Feldway** and **Justice King Michael** betray Emperor Rudra, summoning billions of phantom angels from the spiritual realm to destroy the Cardinal World and awaken the World-Eater Dragon **Ivaraje**.
* **The Octagram United**:
  * Guy Crimson, Milim Nava, Rimuru Tempest, Luminous, Leon, and the Twelve Guardian Lords mount the supreme multidimensional defense across the cosmos.
  * Rimuru obliterates Michael with *Turn Null (Nihility Collapse)*, absorbing the remaining Virtue Series Ultimate Skills into his infinite archive.
* **The Eternal Golden Era**:
  * Feldway is banished to the dimensional abyss; the universe is preserved, and Rimuru Tempest stands as the supreme protector, cultural luminary, and divine sovereign of the **Cardinal World**.
