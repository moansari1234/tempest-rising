# 🌲 ARC 2: THE FOREST OF JURA & THE GOBLIN SETTLEMENT
### *(ジュラの森平定・シズ編 - Jura no Mori Heitei / Shizu-hen)*

---

## 🐺 1. The Starving Goblin Clan & The Direwolf Siege
* **The Encounter**: Exiting the cave, Rimuru encounters a trembling group of small, green-skinned Goblins led by an elderly elder (**Rigurd**). With Veldora's protective aura gone, a ferocious pack of 100 giant **Direwolves** is marching to wipe out the village.
* **The Night Battle**:
  * Rimuru constructs a defensive palisade using *Sticky Threads* and *Steel Threads*.
  * The massive Direwolf Alpha leaps into the air; Rimuru executes a single high-speed *Water Blade*, decapitating the Alpha instantly.
  * Rather than exterminating the pack, Rimuru demands their surrender. The Direwolf pack bows in submission.

---

## 🏷️ 2. The Mass Naming & Metamorphosis
* **Naming the Tribe**: Rimuru names all Goblins and Direwolves. He names the Elder **Rigurd**, the late chief's son **Rigur**, the clever scout **Gobta**, and the Alpha's son **Ranga** (Storm Wolf).
* **The Naming Coma**: Naming hundreds of monsters drains all of Rimuru's magicules, plunging him into a 3-day recovery sleep.
* **The Evolution**:
  * Goblins evolve into towering, athletic **Hobgoblins** and beautiful **Goblinas**.
  * Rigurd becomes an imposing muscle-bound leader.
  * Direwolves evolve into giant, horned **Tempest Star Wolves**, with Ranga residing in Rimuru’s shadow.

---

## ⛏️ 3. Expedition to the Armed Nation of Dwargon
* **The Need for Craftsmen**: To build proper houses, clothing, and weapons, Rimuru, Gobta, and the Star Wolves travel to the underground fortress kingdom of **Dwargon**.
* **The Incident & Prison Encounter**:
  * Rimuru is harassed by guards at the gate. Gobta is jailed, but Rimuru heals severely wounded dwarven miners using his high-grade Full Potions.
  * Word reaches the master blacksmith **Kaijin** and the artisan **Garm brothers**.
* **The Bar Confrontation & King Gazel's Trial**:
  * Corrupt Minister **Vesta** insults Kaijin in an elven butterfly tavern; Kaijin punches Vesta in the face.
  * The Hero King **Gazel Dwargo** presides over the court trial. Recognizing Rimuru's immense inner dragon aura and Kaijin's honor, Gazel exiles Kaijin to Rimuru's care and covertly establishes a secret diplomatic pact with the slime.

---

## 🔥 4. Shizue Izawa & The Conqueror of Flames
* **The Adventurer Trio**: Adventurers **Kabal**, **Eren**, and **Gido** arrive in the village accompanied by a mysterious masked woman, **Shizue Izawa (Shizu-san)**.
* **Shizu's Past**: An Otherworlder summoned from the burning ruins of World War II Tokyo by Demon Lord Leon Cromwell, who bound the Flame Giant **Ifrit** to her body.
* **Ifrit Rampages**:
  * Shizu’s life force wanes, causing Ifrit to break free, engulfing the village in a raging sea of flames.
  * Rimuru engages Ifrit. Thanks to his intrinsic *Thermal Fluctuation Resistance (Flame Immunity)*, Ifrit's hellfire has zero effect on the slime.
  * Rimuru uses *Predator* to swallow Ifrit whole, separating the spirit from Shizu's soul.

---

## 🌸 5. Shizu's Dying Wish & The Human Form
* **The Farewell**: On her deathbed, Shizu recounts her grief and asks Rimuru to eat her body so she doesn't decompose in a world she hated, while hoping he can find Leon Cromwell and check on her 5 students in Ingrassia.
* **Human Mimicry**: Rimuru devours Shizu with *Predator*, acquiring the ability to transform into a beautiful, silver-blue haired human form based on Shizu’s youth.
