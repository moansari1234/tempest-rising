# 💀 ARC 6: THE FALL OF FALMUTH & THE HARVEST FESTIVAL
### *(魔王覚醒・神之怒編 - Maō Kakusei / Megiddo-hen)*

---

## 🩸 1. The Invasion of Tempest & The Anti-Magic Barrier
* **The Falmuth Conspiracy**: Greedy King Edmaris of the Kingdom of Falmuth, seeing Tempest dominate the continent’s silk, potion, and overland trade routes, allies with the Western Holy Church to justify a holy purge of the monster nation.
* **The Double Barrier Trap**:
  * The Church casts the **Holy Purification Barrier (Prison Field)** over Tempest, draining all monsters of 90% of their strength and cutting off communication.
  * Three summoned Otherworlder thugs (**Shogo Taguchi**, **Kyoya Tachibana**, **Kirara Mizutani**) infiltrate the city, provoking riots and mercilessly slaughtering unarmed citizens.
* **Shion’s Sacrifice**: To protect a frightened goblin child while her strength is drained by the barrier, **Shion** throws herself in front of a blade, sacrificing her life along with 100+ innocent Tempest citizens.

---

## 🗡️ 2. The Return of Rimuru & Hinata Sakaguchi's Ambush
* **Hinata's Trap**: Outside Ingrassia, Holy Knight Captain **Hinata Sakaguchi** ambushes Rimuru within an anti-magic boundary. Armed with the holy rapier *Moonlight*, Hinata uses the 7-hit absolute death technique **Dead End Rainbow (七彩終焉刺突)**.
* **The Clone Decoy**: Rimuru uses a magic clone to absorb the lethal 7th strike, escaping underground to rush back to the devastated capital.
* **The Despair in the Central Plaza**:
  * Rimuru finds the central plaza covered in white sheets.
  * He lifts the cloth over Shion's cold, lifeless body. Shuna and Benimaru weep as Rimuru's heart shatters, his aura flaring with cold, terrifying wrath.
* **Eren's Fairy Tale & The Legend of the Demon Lord**:
  * The elf adventurer **Eren** confesses that she is the princess of the Sorcerer's Dynasty Sarion.
  * She tells Rimuru an ancient legend: when the pet dragon of Demon Lord Milim Nava was murdered by humans, Milim massacred the kingdom, absorbed their **10,000 human souls**, and evolved into a **True Demon Lord**, resurrecting her dragon in the process.

---

## ⚡ 3. The Execution of the 20,000-Man Invading Army
* **The Falmuth Vanguard Camp**: King Edmaris and Archbishop Reyhiem assemble 20,000 fully armored knights on the plains outside Tempest, celebrating their anticipated conquest.
* **Hakuro and Geld’s Retribution**:
  * Hakuro beheads Kyoya in a flash, slicing through his *All-Seeing Eye*.
  * Geld mercilessly pulverizes Shogo into paste with bare-handed brawling.
* **MEGIDDO (God’s Wrath - 神之怒)**:
  * Rimuru floats high above the clouds. He conjures tens of thousands of floating water convex lenses, concentrating pure sunlight into supersonic thermal laser beams.
  * Within 5 minutes, 20,000 knights are pierced through their skulls simultaneously with zero noise and zero MP cost.
* **Merciless Devour (Heartless One - 心無者)**:
  * Rimuru casts *Heartless One*, instantly seizing the souls of all surviving soldiers begging for mercy, securing the **20,000 souls** required for his ascension.

---

## 🌌 4. The Harvest Festival (魔王への進化)
* **The Voice of the World Announces**:
  * *« Notice: Harvest Festival has commenced. Evolution from Slime to Demon Slime is in progress... »*
* **The Evolution of Wisdom King Raphael**:
  * During the Harvest Sleep, Great Sage attempts millions of evolutionary permutations, finally sacrificing its own individuality to synthesize the **Ultimate Skill: Wisdom King Raphael (智慧之王)** and **Ultimate Skill: Gluttonous King Beelzebuth (暴食之王)**.
* **Summoning Primordial Black (Noir / Diablo)**:
  * To capture fleeing survivors, Rimuru offers 20,000 human corpses as a sacrifice to summon a demon. The ancient **Primordial Black (Noir)** responds with ecstasy, pledging eternal fealty as Rimuru's head butler under the name **Diablo**.

---

## ✨ 5. The Secret Art of Spiritual Revival & Veldora’s Rebirth
* **Resurrection of the Fallen**:
  * Wisdom King Raphael gathers the dispersed souls of Shion and the 100+ fallen citizens within a massive barrier, infusing them with infinite magicules from Beelzebuth.
  * Shion and all fallen citizens awaken alive, healthy, and spiritually enhanced!
* **The Release of Veldora**:
  * Raphael completes the analysis of *Unlimited Imprisonment*.
  * Rimuru crafts a specialized human male clone body. Veldora’s spiritual core transitions into the clone, resurrecting the **Storm Dragon Veldora** in all his golden-haired, dragon-aura glory!
