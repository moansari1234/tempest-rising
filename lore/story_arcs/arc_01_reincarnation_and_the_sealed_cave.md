# 📖 ARC 1: THE REINCARNATION & THE SEALED CAVE
### *(転生・封印の洞窟編 - Tensho / Fūin no Dōkutsu-hen)*

---

## 🌌 1. The Death of Satoru Mikami in Tokyo
* **The Incident**: Satoru Mikami, a 37-year-old mild-mannered corporate construction contractor in Tokyo, meets his junior colleague Tamura and Tamura’s fiancée Miho on the street. An erratic robber charges at them with a kitchen knife. Satoru throws himself in front of Tamura, taking the fatal stab to the abdomen.
* **The Dying Thoughts & Voice of the World**:
  * As he bleeds out, Satoru complains about the intense heat and blood loss. The Voice of the World interprets his dying desires as skill acquisition parameters:
    * *"It's so hot..."* $\longrightarrow$ **Acquired: Thermal Fluctuation Resistance (Heat/Cold Resistance)**.
    * *"I can't afford to bleed out..."* $\longrightarrow$ **Acquired: Physical Attack Resistance & Bloodless Body (Create Slime Physiology)**.
    * *"If only I could know everything..."* $\longrightarrow$ **Acquired: Unique Skill: Great Sage (大賢者 - Daikenja)**.
    * *"In my next life, I'll devour every pleasure..."* $\longrightarrow$ **Acquired: Unique Skill: Predator (捕食者 - Hoshokusha)**.

---

## 🦇 2. Awakening as a Blue Slime in the Whispering Caverns
* **Sensory Deprivation & Magic Sense**: Satoru wakes up in pitch-black darkness, unable to see, hear, or breathe. Great Sage initiates its first calculation, explaining that his body is an amorphous, gelatinous slime consisting of pure magicules.
* **Herb & Mineral Farming**: Satoru begins aimlessly bouncing around the cavern, using *Predator* to dissolve and store vast fields of rare **Hipokute Herbs** and deposits of high-purity **Magisteel Ore**, synthesizing thousands of healing elixirs in his infinite stomach space.

---

## ⚡ 3. The Encounter with the Storm Dragon Veldora
* **The Meeting**: Bouncing off a massive barrier, Satoru hears a booming telepathic laugh. It is the **Storm Dragon Veldora**, a Catastrophe-class True Dragon who was sealed 300 years earlier by a human Hero using the dimensional magic *Unlimited Imprisonment*.
* **Magic Sense (魔力感知)**: Veldora teaches the slime how to perceive magicules in the atmosphere, granting Satoru sight and spatial awareness for the first time in this new world.
* **The Pact & The Family Name**:
  * Moved by Veldora's loneliness, Satoru promises to help him escape the seal.
  * To establish an equal spiritual bond, they bestow upon each other the shared surname **Tempest**. Veldora names the slime **Rimuru**, becoming **Rimuru Tempest**.
* **Devouring the Imprisonment Seal**:
  * Rimuru uses *Predator* to swallow Veldora along with the entire *Unlimited Imprisonment* barrier into his stomach. Great Sage initiates an immense background calculation to analyze and unravel the seal from within.

---

## ⚔️ 4. Subterranean Combat Trials & Skill Harvest
With the immense dragon aura dispersing, cavern monsters begin emerging from the deep strata:
1. **Giant Bat**: Rimuru uses *Water Blade* to slice it down. Devouring it grants **Ultrasound Waves** (enabling vocal speech and sonar).
2. **Black Spider**: Traps Rimuru with razor webs. Rimuru dissolves the threads and devours it, unlocking **Steel Thread** and **Sticky Thread**.
3. **Evil Centipede**: Attacks with venomous breath. Devouring it grants **Paralysis Breath** and heavy armored skin.
4. **The Tempest Serpent (Leviathan Guardian of the Deep)**:
   * Rank A disaster serpent inhabiting the lower water reservoirs. Attacks with a lethal *Poison Mist*.
   * Rimuru blinds it with *Water Slashes*, binds it in *Steel Thread*, and consumes it with *Predator*.
   * **Acquired**: **Noxious Breath (Poison Mist)** and **Thermal Perception**.

---

## 🚪 5. Breaking Out into the Great Jura Forest
* Rimuru melts the massive adamantine stone gate of the Sealed Cave using *Noxious Breath*.
* He steps into the lush emerald daylight of the **Great Jura Forest**, officially commencing his journey as the sovereign of monsters.
