# 🐖 ARC 3: THE OGRE CLAN & THE ORC DISASTER
### *(豚頭魔王・大樹の盟主編 - Butagashira Maō / Jura Alliance-hen)*

---

## 👹 1. The Ogre Massacre & The Forest Skirmish
* **The Fall of the Ogre Village**: An army of tens of thousands of armored Orcs suddenly invades the sacred Ogre mountain settlement, armed with mysterious high-grade spears and led by a masked majin (**Gelmud**). Only six Ogres survive.
* **The Forest Misunderstanding**:
  * Encountering Rimuru wearing Shizu's anti-magic mask, the Young Ogre Prince assumes Rimuru is the masked majin who orchestrated their clan's genocide.
  * A fierce skirmish erupts: the elder swordmaster **Hakuro** slices Rimuru's arm clean off with blind speed (instantly regenerated), while the heavy hammer warrior **Shion** attacks with brute force.
  * Rimuru unleashes a blinding burst of dragon aura and *Black Lightning*, neutralizing the battle and clearing the misunderstanding over a feast in the village.

---

## 🏮 2. Naming the Kijin (Oni Royalty)
* Pledging temporary allegiance to Rimuru to avenge their clan, the six Ogres accept names:
  1. **Benimaru (Red Flame)** $\longrightarrow$ Red-haired Warrior Prince & Supreme Commander.
  2. **Shion (Purple Violet)** $\longrightarrow$ Greatsword Warrior & Rimuru's fiercely loyal bodyguard/secretary.
  3. **Shuna (Vermilion Flower)** $\longrightarrow$ Holy Shrine Princess & master weaver/magical researcher.
  4. **Souei (Shadow)** $\longrightarrow$ Blue-haired Shinobi & Master of shadow clones/espionage.
  5. **Hakuro (White Feather)** $\longrightarrow$ Blind-eyed Grandmaster Swordsman & combat instructor.
  6. **Kurobe (Black Steel)** $\longrightarrow$ Master divine blacksmith.
* All six undergo profound spiritual evolution, shedding their rough horns to become noble, aristocratic **Kijin (鬼人)**.

---

## 🍃 3. Treyni's Request & The Jura Forest Alliance
* **The Dryad's Arrival**: **Treyni**, guardian spirit of the Great Jura Forest, materializes in Tempest and kneels before Rimuru, requesting that he subjugate the **Orc Lord**, an ominous Calamity-class entity threatening all forest life.
* **The Lizardmen Crisis**:
  * The 200,000 Orc army marches into the marshlands of Lake Siss to devour the Lizardmen tribe.
  * The arrogant Lizardman officer **Gabil** stages a coup d'état against his father, Chief Abil, leading his spearmen into an Orc ambush.
  * Souei rescues Chief Abil, while Gobta and Ranga arrive to save Gabil from being slaughtered.

---

## 💀 4. The Battle of the Marshlands & Gelmud's Fall
* **The Tempest Vanguard**:
  * Benimaru incinerates thousands of Orcs with *Hellflare*.
  * Shion cleaves through armored vanguard lines with her Odachi *Gorikimaru*.
  * Hakuro beheads the Orc generals with blinding *Crest Water Slashes*.
* **Gelmud's Intervention**:
  * The Demon Puppeteer Gelmud arrives, furious that his puppets are failing. He attacks Gabil and orders Orc Lord Geld to devour Rimuru.
  * Geld turns on Gelmud, beheads him, and eats his corpse, triggering his dark evolution into **Orc Disaster Geld (Special S Rank)** with the world-consuming skill **Starving Ones (飢餓者)**.

---

## 👑 5. Predator vs. Starving Ones: The Clash of Sins
* **The Final Duel**: Rimuru shifts into his Slime form and engulfs Orc Disaster Geld in a direct battle of metaphysical consumption: Rimuru’s *Predator* against Geld’s *Starving Ones*.
* **Bearing the Sins of the Starving**:
  * Entering Geld’s spiritual consciousness, Rimuru witnesses the famine that drove the Orc king to despair and cannibalism to save his people.
  * Rimuru comforts Geld's dying soul: *"Rest easy. I will devour all your sins and your hunger. Your people will survive under my care."*
* **The Aftermath & High Orc Integration**:
  * Rimuru names the late king's successor **Geld (II)** and grants amnesty to all 150,000 surviving Orcs, incorporating them as the supreme construction and civil engineering corps of the **Jura Tempest Federation**.
