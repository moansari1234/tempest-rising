# 🐉 ARC 4: THE DESTROYER MILIM & CHARYBDIS
### *(魔王来訪・暴風大妖渦編 - Maō Raihō / Charybdis-hen)*

---

## 🍯 1. The Descent of Demon Lord Milim Nava
* **The Crimson Meteor**: A supersonic shockwave crashes into the outskirts of Tempest. It is the ancient Catastrophe Demon Lord **Milim Nava (The Destroyer)**, daughter of the Creator God Veldanava.
* **The Honey Diplomacy**:
  * Benimaru, Shion, and Ranga launch desperate high-tier attacks that shatter against Milim's passive dragon aura without leaving a scratch.
  * Before Milim obliterates the forest with a punch, Rimuru leaps forward and shoves a massive spoonful of sweet bee honey into her mouth.
  * Overwhelmed by the delicious sweetness, Milim immediately ceases all hostility and declares Rimuru her **"Best Friend / Sworn Sister"**.

---

## 🤝 2. King Gazel's Official Visit & The Eurazania Emissaries
* **The Armed Nation Treaty**: King Gazel Dwargo marches to Tempest with his Pegasus Knights. Seeing Rimuru comfortably ordering around an ancient Demon Lord, Gazel formally signs an official mutual non-aggression and economic trade treaty between Dwargon and Tempest.
* **The Beastketeers Arrival**: Emissaries from Beast King **Carrion** of the Animal Kingdom Eurazania arrive, including the hotheaded **Phobio (Leopard Lord)**.
* **Phobio's Humiliation**:
  * Phobio attacks Rigurd in a fit of rage. Milim knocks Phobio across the horizon with a single restrained slap.
  * Humiliated and burning with vengeance, Phobio leaves the village in disgrace.

---

## 🦈 3. The Rebirth of Charybdis (Calamity of the Skies)
* **The Clowns' Manipulation**: **Footman** and **Tear** of the Moderate Harlequin Alliance ambush Phobio, tempting him with promises of supreme power to avenge his humiliation.
* **The Sky Disaster**:
  * Phobio allows himself to be consumed as the core for **Charybdis (暴風大妖渦)**, an ancient colossal sky-whale formed from Veldora’s ambient magicules.
  * Charybdis awakens in the stratosphere alongside 13 colossal flying **Megalodon** shark escorts, homing in on Tempest.

---

## ⚔️ 4. The 10-Hour Aerial War
* **The Allied Defense**:
  * Tempest deploys all defensive divisions: Gabil's Dragonewts engage the Megalodons in dogfights; Kaijin's magicule cannons barrage the sky; Benimaru's *Hellflare* and Rimuru's *Black Flame* bombard Charybdis.
* **Magic Interference (魔力妨害)**:
  * Charybdis' passive aura deflects 90% of all direct magic spells.
  * The battle turns into a grueling 10-hour war of attrition. While Tempest sustains zero casualties thanks to Rimuru's healing potions, Charybdis regenerates constantly.

---

## 💥 5. Drago Buster & Phobio's Liberation
* **Milim Steps In**:
  * Rimuru notices that Charybdis is primarily targeting Milim. Rimuru permits Milim to attack on the condition that she does not harm the forest or kill Phobio inside.
  * Milim channels a mere 1% fraction of her power into **Drago Buster (竜皇覇手)**, vaporizing Charybdis' colossal body in a single blinding blast of light while leaving Phobio intact.
* **Devouring the Core**:
  * Rimuru uses *Predator* to dissolve the remnants of Charybdis and purge the curse from Phobio’s soul, saving his life.
  * Demon Lord Carrion arrives to apologize for Phobio's foolishness, establishing formal diplomatic and agricultural trade relations between **Tempest** and **Eurazania**.
