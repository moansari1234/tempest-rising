# 🏫 ARC 5: ROYAL CAPITAL INGRASSIA & THE SPIRIT SANCTUARY
### *(王都生活・精霊棲家編 - Ōto Seikatsu / Seirei Sumika-hen)*

---

## 🏙️ 1. Journey to the Kingdom of Ingrassia
* **Fulfilling Shizu's Wish**: Rimuru entrusts the daily governance of Tempest to Rigurd, Benimaru, and Shuna, and departs on Ranga toward the human cultural capital, the **Kingdom of Ingrassia**, to find Shizu-san’s five summoned Otherworlder students.
* **Meeting Yuuki Kagurazaka**:
  * Rimuru meets the Grandmaster of the Free Guild, **Yuuki Kagurazaka**, an Otherworlder from Japan.
  * To prove his origins and intentions, Rimuru creates and gift-wraps manga volumes (including *Dragon Ball*, *One Piece*, and *Shonen Jump*) materialized using *Predator* and paper pulp alchemy.
  * Yuuki warmly welcomes Rimuru and appoints him as the special instructor for the troubled Class S at the Freedom Academy.

---

## 🎒 2. The Five Summoned Otherworlder Children
* **The Children**: **Kenya Misaki** (fire attribute), **Ryota Sekiguchi** (water attribute), **Gale Gibson** (earth attribute), **Alice Rondo** (spatial puppeteer), and **Chloe Aubert** (quiet girl clutching Shizu's storybook).
* **The Looming Death Sentence**:
  * Great Sage discovers that all five children were incomplete Otherworlder summons: their immature child bodies cannot contain the massive, unstable magicules dwelling in their spiritual cores.
  * Within three to five years, the overflowing magicules will rupture their organs and burn them alive from within.
* **The Solution**: Just as Shizue survived for decades because Demon Lord Leon fused her with the high-tier fire spirit Ifrit, the five children must be bonded with **Superior Elemental Spirits** to safely regulate their internal magicules.

---

## 🧚 3. The Queen of Spirits & The Labyrinth Sanctuary
* **The Quest for the Sanctuary of Spirits**: Rimuru and the children travel to the Ulgracia Kingdom to enter the ancient, mystical **Dwelling of the Spirits (精霊の棲家)**.
* **Encountering Fairy Demon Lord Ramiris**:
  * Deep within the labyrinth, they encounter the Fairy of the Labyrinth, **Ramiris**, a former Queen of Spirits who fell into Demon Lord status after calming Milim Nava's first ancient rage.
  * Ramiris deploys her giant bio-mechanical Elemental Magisteel Golem to test Rimuru. Rimuru melts the Golem with a concentrated thermal blast, promising to build her an even cooler replacement golem if she helps the children.

---

## ✨ 4. The Spirit Fusion Ritual
* **Kenya, Ryota, Gale, and Alice**:
  * One by one, the children pray at the elemental altar.
  * Kenya bonds with a **Superior Light Spirit**; Gale bonds with an **Earth Giant Spirit**; Alice bonds with a **Sky Spatial Sylph**; Ryota bonds with a hybrid **Water-Wind Spirit**.
* **Chloe Aubert & The Mysterious Spiritual Entity**:
  * When Chloe steps up to pray, the space-time continuum fluctuates. A mysterious, colossal, humanoid spiritual entity glowing with celestial light materializes from the future.
  * The entity kisses Rimuru on the lips before fusing directly into Chloe's soul, completely stabilizing her magicules (later revealed to be the spiritual core of the Time-Traveling Hero **Chronoa**).

---

## 🎁 5. The Mask Passed Down & The Farewell
* With all five children saved and their lifespan crisis permanently resolved, Rimuru bestows **Shizu’s Anti-Magic Mask** upon young Chloe as a protective talisman.
* Rimuru bids farewell to the children and begins his journey back toward Tempest, unaware that an apocalyptic conspiracy orchestrated by the Kingdom of Falmuth and the Western Holy Church has already trapped his city in a genocidal barrier.
