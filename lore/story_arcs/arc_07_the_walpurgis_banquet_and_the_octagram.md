# 🎭 ARC 7: THE WALPURGIS BANQUET & THE OCTAGRAM
### *(魔王達の宴・八星魔王編 - Walpurgis / Octagram-hen)*

---

## 🎪 1. Clayman's Desperate Gambit
* **The Puppet Master's Conspiracy**: After the annihilation of the Falmuth army, the Marionette Demon Lord **Clayman** realizes his plans are unraveling.
* **The Invasion of Eurazania**:
  * Clayman frames Rimuru for Carrion's supposed betrayal and sends a 30,000-man puppet army to pillage the Animal Kingdom **Eurazania** to harvest human/beast souls for his own True Demon Lord awakening.
* **The Two-Pronged Counter-Offensive**:
  * **Eurazania Front**: Benimaru leads the Tempest Allied Army alongside Albis and Suphia, completely crushing Clayman’s puppet army, while Hakuro and Souei infiltrate Clayman's castle with the three Beastketeers.
  * **Walpurgis Front**: Rimuru, accompanied by **Shion** and **Ranga**, attends the sacred Demon Lord assembly (**Walpurgis**) hosted in Guy Crimson's extra-dimensional palace.

---

## 🏛️ 2. The Walpurgis Council of Demon Lords
* **The Attendees**:
  1. **Guy Crimson** (Lord of Darkness / Primordial Red - Chairman)
  2. **Milim Nava** (The Destroyer - pretending to be mind-controlled by Clayman)
  3. **Ramiris** (Fairy of the Labyrinth)
  4. **Dagruel** (Giant Titan of the Deserts)
  5. **Dino** (Fallen Angel / Sleeping Ruler)
  6. **Luminous Valentine** (Vampire Queen - posing as a maid behind her body double Roy)
  7. **Leon Cromwell** (Platinum Saber)
  8. **Frey** (Sky Queen / Harpy Sovereign)
  9. **Carrion** (Beast Master)
  10. **Clayman** (The Accuser)
  11. **Rimuru Tempest** (The Challenger)

---

## 🥊 3. The Humiliation of Clayman & Milim's Oscar-Worthy Act
* **Clayman's Accusations**: Clayman accuses Rimuru of murdering Carrion and violating the Great Jura Forest non-interference pact.
* **Guy Crimson's Verdict**: Guy declares that in the realm of Demon Lords, words are meaningless—truth is decided through combat: *Rimuru vs. Clayman's forces*.
* **Shion Pulverizes Clayman**:
  * Clayman attempts to fight with his *Demon Wires*; Shion blocks every strike bare-handed, beating Clayman within an inch of his life and snapping his limbs like twigs.
* **Veldora Interrupts & Milim's Laugh**:
  * Milim attacks Rimuru; Rimuru struggles until **Veldora** bursts through a dimensional rift demanding the next chapter of the *Dragon Ball* manga!
  * Veldora distracts Milim by exchanging punches. Milim bursts into laughter, revealing she was **never mind-controlled** and was only pretending to expose Clayman’s backers!

---

## 🌀 4. The Execution of Clayman & Beelzebuth's Meal
* **Awakened Clayman**: In absolute terror, Clayman consumes all the spiritual energy stored in his dragon core to force a pseudo-Awakening (*Crazy Pierrot Clayman*).
* **The Final Clash**:
  * Clayman fires his ultimate strike, the **Dragon Buster Leyline Cannon**.
  * Rimuru activates **Ultimate Skill: Gluttonous King Beelzebuth (暴食之王)**, opening a pitch-black cosmic singularity that swallows the blast, Clayman’s body, his skills, and his soul in a single bite.
* **Memory Extraction**: Beelzebuth analyzes Clayman's soul memories, uncovering the grand conspiracy orchestrated by **Yuuki Kagurazaka** and the **Eastern Empire**.

---

## ⭐ 5. The Birth of the Octagram (八星魔王)
* **The Resignation of Carrion & Frey**: Realizing their strength is dwarfed by the True Demon Lords, Carrion and Frey voluntarily step down to become vassals under Milim Nava’s domain.
* **The Name of the Eight Stars**:
  * With the Ten Great Demon Lords reduced to eight members, Guy Crimson tasks Rimuru with inventing a new title.
  * Looking up at the starry cosmos, Rimuru proposes **The Octagram (Eight Star Demon Lords - 八星魔王)**.
* **Universal Recognition**: The Voice of the World broadcasts the ascension of **Rimuru Tempest: The New Star (Chaos Creator)** as an official member of the Octagram.
