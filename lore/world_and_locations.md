# 🗺️ TENSURA: WORLD GEOGRAPHY, REALMS & LOCATIONS COMPENDIUM

---

## 🌍 The Cardinal World (基軸世界 - Central World)

The primary physical plane in the multiverse, anchored by the divine will of the Creator God **Star Dragon King Veldanava**. Because of its high dimensional anchor, magicules and spiritual matter can materialize into permanent physical forms.

---

## 🌲 The Great Jura Forest (ジュラの森大森林)

A vast, untamed emerald wilderness stretching across the center of the continent, historically protected from human encroachment by the divine aura of the Storm Dragon Veldora.

### 1. 🦇 The Sealed Cave (封印の洞窟 / Whispering Caverns)
* **Significance**: The subterranean cavern where Veldora was imprisoned in *Unlimited Imprisonment* for 300 years, and the birthplace where Satoru Mikami reincarnated as **Rimuru the Slime**.
* **Flora & Minerals**:
  * **Magisteel Ore (魔鋼)**: Precious subterranean iron ore saturated with thousands of years of dragon magicules.
  * **Hipokute Herbs (ヒポクテ草)**: Rare luminescent violet flora used to brew high-potency restorative potions and elixirs.
* **Creatures**: Tempest Serpent, Evil Centipedes, Giant Bats, Black Spiders.

### 2. 🏰 The Jura Tempest Federation (Capital City Rimuru)
* **Founder**: Rimuru Tempest.
* **Architecture**: A sprawling, paved metropolis blending Dwarven masonry, High Elf aesthetics, monster cooperation, and modern Japanese civic planning (hot springs, sewer systems, paved stone boulevards, glass lanterns).
* **Population**: Over 500,000 monsters and demi-humans including Goblins, Kijin, High Orcs, Dragonewts, Dwarves, and beastmen living in harmonious civilization.

### 3. 🐊 Lake Siss & The Marshlands (シス湖)
* **Inhabitants**: The Lizardmen tribe led by Abil and Gabil.
* **Features**: Submerged stone caverns, aquatic reed fortresses, and spawning grounds for subterranean giant catfish.

## 🌐 Sovereign Nations & Foreign Superpowers

### ⛏️ 1. Armed Nation of Dwargon (武装国家ドワルゴン)
* **Ruler**: Hero King Gazel Dwargo.
* **Capital**: The Central Cavern Fortress of St. Darom.
* **Description**: An ancient underground empire carved into a mountain range, home to 100,000 Master Dwarves, blacksmiths, and the elite Pegasus Knights.

### 🧝 2. Sorcerer's Dynasty Sarion (魔導王朝サリオン)
* **Ruler**: Empress Elmesia El-Ru Sarion.
* **Description**: A 2,000-year-old ancient High Elven empire powered by ancient magical magi-tech and the Holy Tree. A close economic and military ally of Tempest.

### ✝️ 3. Holy Empire Ruberios (神聖法皇国ルベリオス)
* **True Ruler**: Demon Lord Luminous Valentine.
* **Public Figurehead**: Pope Louis Valentine & Holy Knight Captain Hinata Sakaguchi.
* **Description**: A strictly monotheistic nation worshipping the *"God Luminas"*. In secret, humans are protected within the barrier in exchange for peaceful coexistence with vampire aristocracy.

### 🎖️ 4. The Eastern Empire (Nasca Namrium Ulmeria)
* **Ruler**: Emperor Rudra Nam Ul Nasca.
* **Description**: A colossal militaristic superpower on the eastern continent boasting magi-tech airships, mechanised tank divisions, and millions of cybernetically and magically enhanced soldiers.

### ❄️ 5. The Frozen Continent (氷土の大陸)
* **Ruler**: Lord of Darkness **Guy Crimson** and True Dragon **Velzard**.
* **Description**: A permafrost wasteland at the northern pole where Guy Crimson's crimson demon palace stands amidst absolute zero blizzards.
