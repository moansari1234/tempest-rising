# 👥 TENSURA: CHARACTERS & ALLIES COMPENDIUM

---

## 👑 The Protagonist & Core Entity

### 🔵 Rimuru Tempest (リムル＝テンペスト)
* **Original Identity**: Satoru Mikami (37-year-old corporate contractor from Tokyo, Japan).
* **Species Evolution**:
  1. *Slime (Monster)* $\longrightarrow$ Reincarnated in the Sealed Cave with dense magicule absorption.
  2. *Demon Slime (Viscous Dragon Demonoid)* $\longrightarrow$ Awakened as a True Demon Lord after the Harvest Festival.
  3. *Ultimate Slime (Viscous Draconic Demon God)* $\longrightarrow$ Acquired the dragon factor of True Dragons Veldora and Velgrynd.
* **Titles**: Great Demon Lord, Ruler of Monsters, Chaos Creator, Leader of the Jura Tempest Federation, Octagram Member (*Newbie*).
* **Core Characteristics**:
  * Gelatinous azure body with total cellular fluidity, immune to physical cuts and piercing strikes.
  * Capable of shifting into a serene androgynous human form (based on Shizu Izawa's genetic memory).
  * Possesses the infinite storage stomach space and analysis capabilities of the Predator $\to$ Gluttony $\to$ Beelzebuth line.

## 🧠 The Internal Partner: Voice & Intelligence

### 🧬 Great Sage $\longrightarrow$ Wisdom King Raphael $\longrightarrow$ Manas: Ciel
* **Great Sage (大賢者 - Daikenja)**:
  * Unique Skill born from Satoru's dying desire to understand and analyze everything.
  * Acts as Rimuru's internal supercomputer: rapid thought calculation (1,000x speed), parallel processing, analytical appraisal, and automated chantless magic invocation.
* **Wisdom King Raphael (智慧之王 - Rafaeru)**:
  * Evolved during the True Demon Lord Harvest Festival.
  * Upgrades thought acceleration to **1,000,000x**, unlocks complete matter manipulation, parallel computation, universal law manipulation, and autonomous combat execution.
* **Manas: Ciel (神智核 - Shieru)**:
  * Awakened with true self-awareness and emotion when Rimuru bestows upon Raphael the name **Ciel** (meaning *"Sky"* / *"Heaven"*).
  * Manages all of Rimuru's skills, subordinates' soul corridors, skill synthesis, and divine power routing.

## 🐉 The True Dragons (竜種 - Ryoushu)

### ⚡ Veldora Tempest (ヴェルドラ＝テンペスト)
* **Species**: True Dragon (Storm Dragon - 暴風竜).
* **Title**: The Catastrophe Dragon of the Great Jura Forest.
* **History**:
  * Sealed 300 years prior by the Hero (Chloe Aubert) using *Unlimited Imprisonment*.
  * Encountered Rimuru inside the Sealed Cave; shared the family name **Tempest** and allowed Rimuru to devour his seal to analyze and release him.
* **Abilities**: *Storm Magic*, *Ultimate Skill: Investigation King Faust*, *Death-Heralding Winds*, *Black Lightning Torrent*, *Probability Manipulation*.

### 🐉 Milim Nava (ミリム・ナーヴァ)
* **Species**: Dragonoid (Daughter of the Star Dragon King Veldanava and Lucia).
* **Title**: Destroyer (破壊の暴君 - Destroyer), Ancient Demon Lord of the Octagram.
* **Personality**: Cheerful, honey-loving, childlike, yet wields infinite stardust magicules that can obliterate continents with *Drago Nova*.
* **Status**: Rimuru's declared *"Best Friend (Sworn Sister)"*.

## 🏛️ Allies & Forest Inhabitants

* **Rigurd & Gobta**:
  * **Rigurd**: Prime Minister of Tempest, oversaw the village transition from mud huts to a metropolis.
  * **Gobta**: Genius goblin comic-relief who accidentally mastered *Shadow Step* and defeated high-tier champions with dumb luck.
* **Treyni & The Dryads**:
  * Forest spirits who appointed Rimuru as the Guardian Chancellor of the Great Jura Forest.
* **King Gazel Dwargo (ガゼル・ドワルゴ)**:
  * Hero King of the Armed Nation of Dwargon. Rimuru's martial arts brother (under Hakuro) and first sovereign diplomatic ally.
* **Ramiris (ラミリス)**:
  * Former Queen of Spirits, now an ancient fairy Demon Lord. Constructed the **100-Floor Tempest Dungeon Labyrinth** with Rimuru.
