## 🌌 Fundamental Universe Mechanics

### 1. Magicules (魔素 - Maso)
* **Definition**: The fundamental subatomic energy particle of the spiritual and magical realm. Magicules permeate the atmosphere, saturate soil and flora, and coalesce within living beings as magical aura.
* **Magicule Density**: Extremely dense in untamed areas like the **Sealed Cave** and the **Great Jura Forest** due to the passive aura emission of the **Storm Dragon Veldora**.
* **Magicule Reservoir (Max MP)**: Determines an entity's spellcasting capacity, physical durability, aura pressure, and evolutionary potential.

### 2. The Voice of the World (世界の言葉 - Sekai no Kotoba)
* An omnipresent, universal cosmic broadcast system that governs the laws of reality.
* Announces major universal phenomena, including:
  * **Species Evolution** (e.g. *« Notice: Evolution to Demon Slime has commenced »*)
  * **Skill Acquisition & Synthesis** (e.g. *« Unique Skill: Predator has evolved into Ultimate Skill: Beelzebuth »*)
  * **Harvest Festival Triggering** upon consuming 10,000 human souls for True Demon Lord ascension.

### 3. The Soul & The Naming System
* **Naming (名付け - Nazuke)**: Bestowing a formal name upon a monster permanently consumes a portion of the namer's magicules, writing the name onto the target's soul gene.
* **Effects**: Triggers rapid, drastic evolutionary metamorphosis (e.g., Goblins $\to$ Hobgoblins/Goblinas, Ogres $\to$ Kijin).
* **Soul Corridor (魂の回廊 - Tamashii no Kairou)**: An unbreakable spiritual tether linking Rimuru to his named subordinates, allowing bidirectional magicule sharing, thought communication, and resurrection under Ultimate Skill authority.
