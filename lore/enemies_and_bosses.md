# 👹 TENSURA: ENEMIES, MONSTERS & BOSS COMPENDIUM

---

## 🐉 Early Cavern Monsters (The Whispering Caverns)

During Rimuru's early days after waking up in the **Sealed Cave of Veldora**, he absorbed several high-density subterranean monsters that granted him his foundational combat arsenal:

### 1. 🐉 Tempest Serpent (テンペスト・サーペント)
* **Threat Rank**: Rank A (Hazard Class).
* **Habitat**: Lower Magisteel subterranean aquifers of the Whispering Caverns.
* **Abilities**:
  * *Noxious Breath (Toxic Mist)*: Corrosive poison cloud capable of dissolving flesh and steel in seconds.
  * *Magicule Vision*: Heat and magical particle detection in pitch-black darkness.
  * *Water Current Propelling*: Extreme slithering speed across subterranean rivers.
* **Absorbed by Rimuru**: Granted *Poison Mist (Noxious Breath)* and *Thermal Perception*.

### 2. 🐛 Evil Centipede (エビル・センティピード)
* **Threat Rank**: Rank B+ (Hazard Class).
* **Abilities**: *Paralysis Breath*, *Armored Carapace*.
* **Absorbed by Rimuru**: Granted *Paralysis Breath* and hard chitin armor density.

### 3. 🕷️ Giant Black Spider (ブラック・スパイダー)
* **Threat Rank**: Rank B (Hazard Class).
* **Abilities**: *Steel Thread Manipulation*, *Sticky Web Traps*.
* **Absorbed by Rimuru**: Granted *Steel Thread* and *Sticky Thread* (the origin of Rimuru's and Souei's thread-slicing combat style).

### 4. 🦇 Giant Vampire Bat (ジャイアント・バット)
* **Threat Rank**: Rank C+ (Normal Class).
* **Abilities**: *Ultrasound Waves*, *Flight Echolocation*.
* **Absorbed by Rimuru**: Combined with magicules to produce Rimuru's *Water Blade (High-Pressure Hydro Slashes)* soundwave harmonics and vocal reproduction.

## ⚔️ Major Arc Antagonists & Catastrophe Bosses

### 👑 1. Orc Disaster Geld (I) (オーク・ディザスター ゲルド)
* **Threat Rank**: Disaster Class (Rank S).
* **Lore**: Starved and driven mad by the drought in the Orbic Steppes, the Demon Puppeteer Gelmud named Geld and granted him the **Unique Skill: Starving Ones (飢餓者 - Kiga mono)**.
* **Unique Trait**:
  * Every monster devoured by the 200,000 Orc army shared its strength with Geld; in return, the army was freed from pain and exhaustion.
* **Climax**: Defeated in an epic duel by Rimuru, who used *Predator* to shoulder the entire Orc race's sins and starvation, liberating Geld's soul and naming his successor.

---

### 🦈 2. Charybdis (暴風大妖渦 - Karybdis)
* **Threat Rank**: Calamity / Disaster Class (Rank S).
* **Lore**: A colossal sky-whale formed from dense concentrations of Veldora's ambient magicules, sealed in the spiritual realm and summoned by Phobio.
* **Arsenal**:
  * *Magic Interference*: Completely nullifies and absorbs standard magical attacks in a 10km radius.
  * *Gravity Disruption*: Summons Megalodon shark escorts and rains explosive scales from the stratosphere.
  * *Ultra-Speed Regeneration*: Heals massive wounds instantaneously.
* **Climax**: Rimuru and the Tempest Army fought a 10-hour battle of attrition before Milim Nava annihilated it with a restrained flick of *Drago Buster*.

---

### 🎭 3. Clayman (The Marionette Master - クレイマン)
* **Threat Rank**: Special S (Demon Lord Seed $\to$ Awakened Clayman).
* **Lore**: Member of the Moderate Harlequin Alliance and puppet of the Eastern Empire. Conspired the Falmuth invasion and attempted to awaken as a True Demon Lord by massacring the Animal Kingdom Eurazania.
* **Arsenal**:
  * *Marionette Dance (Demon Wire)*: Hijacks enemy nervous systems.
  * *Leyline Manipulation & Dragon Buster Core*: Channels compressed leyline death rays.
* **Climax**: Publicly challenged by Rimuru during the **Walpurgis Council of Demon Lords**. Rimuru humiliated him, devoured his soul with *Beelzebuth*, and read his memories to expose the Eastern Empire conspiracy.

---

### ✝️ 4. The Kingdom of Falmuth & The Holy Knights
* **King Edmaris & Archbishop Reyhiem**:
  * Greedy monarch and corrupt church prelates who launched an unprovoked surprise invasion on Tempest with anti-magic barriers, slaughtering 100+ innocent citizens including Shion.
* **Otherworlder Assailants (Shogo, Kyoya, Kirara)**:
  * Ruthless summoned humans with cheat skills (*Berserker*, *All-Seeing Eye*, *Severer*). Decisively executed by Geld and Hakuro.
* **The Response: MEGIDDO (God's Wrath - 神之怒)**:
  * Rimuru channeled pure sunlight through tens of thousands of floating water convex lenses, firing thousands of supersonic thermal laser beams simultaneously, wiping out the 20,000-man Falmuth army in under 5 minutes to trigger his Harvest Festival.

---

### 🎖️ 5. The Eastern Empire (Empire of Nasca Namrium)
* **Emperor Rudra Nam Ul Nasca**:
  * Ancient Hero and counterpart to Guy Crimson. Wielded the Ultimate Skill *Armageddon (Justice King Michael)* summoning thousands of angels.
* **Velgrynd (The Scorch Dragon - 灼熱竜)**:
  * True Dragon sibling. Accelerated thermal energy and reality-piercing *Cardinal Burning Flame*. Subdued and freed by Rimuru and Ciel.
* **Imperial Guard Champions**:
  * **Lt. Gen. Kondo**: Wielded *Judgement Bullet* capable of destroying spiritual cores.
  * **Damrada**: Champion of the Secret Fist and master of spiritual martial arts.

---

### 🌌 6. The Primordial Cosmic Threat: Feldway & Ivaraje
* **Feldway (Leader of the Phantoms & Seven Angels of Origin)**:
  * The first creation of God Veldanava. Betrayed the world out of grief for God's disappearance, seeking to awaken the World-Destroying Dragon **Ivaraje** to reset reality.
